Before you decide

Common questions · Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Short answers to help you understand the process. Your final recommendation needs your bill details and a site assessment.

How much subsidy can I get? / Kitni subsidy milegi?

The amount depends on the current scheme rules, system capacity, eligibility and application requirements. We do not promise a fixed subsidy. Please verify the latest details on the official PM Surya Ghar portal.

Will subsidy be deducted from the installation price?

Do not assume so. Ask for the payment schedule in writing and follow the current official scheme process for any subsidy claim or disbursement. The final timing and approval are not controlled by the installer.

Which solar system size is right for my home?

It depends on your electricity consumption, daytime use, usable roof area, shade, sanctioned load and budget. Your monthly bill is a useful starting point, but it is not enough by itself to finalise the design.

Will my electricity bill become zero?

Not necessarily. The result depends on generation, when you use electricity, tariff and applicable metering/billing rules. Treat savings estimates as estimates, not a guarantee.

What should I check in the quotation?

Ask for panel and inverter brand/model, capacity, structure, wiring, earthing, protection devices, taxes, meter/DISCOM responsibilities, payment milestones, exclusions and warranty terms in writing.

What does the panel warranty cover?

Warranty differs by manufacturer, model and type of warranty. Ask for the actual warranty document and check coverage, performance terms, exclusions and how to make a claim. Do not rely on a generic “25-year warranty” label alone.

Can I ask questions before deciding?

Yes. Aap pehle subsidy process, estimated budget, roof suitability ya equipment ke baare mein poochh sakte hain. An enquiry does not commit you to buying a system.
